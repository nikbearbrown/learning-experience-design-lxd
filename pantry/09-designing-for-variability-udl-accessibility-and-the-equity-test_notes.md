## A. Conceptual foundations

### Accessibility is a baseline obligation independent of learning-effect debates
Accessibility is a baseline obligation to remove barriers to access. It should not depend on proving higher test scores.

**Common misconception:** Accessibility changes need learning-gain proof before they are required.

**Worked example:** Use the studio project. Ask what design decision changes if this concept is true. Then create a before/after artifact and a one-sentence Evidence Disclosure. For this chapter, the simplest case is: Adaptive platform routes struggling students into low-ceiling remedial loops.

### UDL 3.0 is useful but broad and evidence-complex
UDL 3.0 helps designers anticipate variability and systemic barriers. Evidence claims require care because interventions are broad, bundled, and context-dependent.

**Common misconception:** The method is a checklist item rather than a decision tool.

**Worked example:** Use the studio project. Ask what design decision changes if this concept is true. Then create a before/after artifact and a one-sentence Evidence Disclosure. For this chapter, the simplest case is: Adaptive platform routes struggling students into low-ceiling remedial loops.

**Source(s):** See priority sources below.

### Personalization can reproduce inequity through tracking
A learning persona should encode prior knowledge, misconceptions, constraints, goals, task value, and motivation. Stock-photo personas with demographic filler produce false empathy.

**Common misconception:** Personas can be invented from intuition or generated by AI without learner contact.

## E. Teaching considerations

**Likely sticking point:** Students will tend to turn this chapter into a checklist. Force them to name the evidence status, the learner data source, and the decision that changes.

**Exercise ideas:**
- Diagnose a flawed artifact from the opening case and label the evidence problem.
- Apply the chapter method to the Track A statistics course.
- Track B: apply the method to the student's own project and write a short Evidence Disclosure.

**LLM guardrail:** Permit AI to generate alternatives or critique clarity, but require the student to provide the evidence label, learner-data source, and final design judgment. Do not let AI replace learner contact, delayed assessment, or ethical review.
