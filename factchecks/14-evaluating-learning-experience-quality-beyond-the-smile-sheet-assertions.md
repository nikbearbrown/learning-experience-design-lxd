### EVIDENCE — CONFIRMED (direction); coefficients UNVERIFIED (exact)
**Assertion type:** BASIC
**Sentence:** "The classic meta-analytic finding is blunt: reactions to training correlate weakly, near zero by some estimates, with how much was actually learned (Alliger et al. 1997 [verify exact coefficients against the primary paper])." Also: "the Alliger et al. (1997) meta-analysis found reaction measures correlate only weakly with learning measures."
**Claim checked:** Alliger et al. 1997 reaction-learning correlations are weak / near zero.
**Site visited:** onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1744-6570.1997.tb00911.x; semanticscholar.org; apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/ADA368508.pdf.
**Finding:** Study CONFIRMED (Alliger, Tannenbaum, Bennett, Traver, & Shotland, 1997, *Personnel Psychology*, 50, 341–358; 34 studies, 115 correlations). Direction CONFIRMED: **affective** reaction measures correlate weakly with learning. IMPORTANT NUANCE the chapter should retain: the paper's headline contribution is that **utility-type** reactions correlate *more strongly* with learning and transfer than affective reactions do — so "reactions correlate near zero with learning" is true only of the affective subtype, not reactions wholesale. The chapter's hedge ("near zero by *some* estimates") is what keeps it accurate; do not remove it. The exact coefficients (commonly cited around r≈.07 affective-to-immediate-learning) could not be pulled from the primary PDF within budget — keep the [verify] flag or cite a secondary source that reports the table.
**Expert review needed:** Yes (light) — a measurement specialist should confirm the exact coefficient and ensure the affective/utility distinction is not flattened. The chapter's two-clause framing is already careful; the risk is only if a later edit drops the hedge.
**Suggested reference:** Alliger, G. M., Tannenbaum, S. I., Bennett, W., Traver, H., & Shotland, A. (1997). A meta-analysis of the relations among training criteria. *Personnel Psychology*, 50, 341–358.
**Notes:** Inline flag NOT inserted because the sentence already carries its own [verify] marker and the claim is hedged; this is a "keep the hedge" finding, not a contradiction.
